Original A architecture and training data

Three matched seeds were trained for each of shaped and unshaped A recipes. The result is mixed: LIVE improves, LIVE clean10 declines, and CSIQ is essentially unchanged. These development comparisons do not establish a general improvement over the serving A model.

Joint product shaping is a useful candidate initialization, but this study did not establish one reusable per-feature bank across seeds. The frozen-bank trials use seed-matched learned prefixes. A universally good default bank requires a separate transfer test.
