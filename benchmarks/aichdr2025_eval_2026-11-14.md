# AIC-HDR2025: zensim A against the dataset's published metric scores (2026-11-14)

**Data.** [jpeg-aic/AIC-HDR2025](https://github.com/jpeg-aic/AIC-HDR2025) commit `33ab64e5`, released
2026-11-14 (QoMEX 2025, [arXiv:2506.12505](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12505)). 5 HDR sources (840×944,
Rec. 2100 PQ, 10-bit in 16-bit PNG) × 4 codecs (AVIF, JPEG AI, JPEG XL, JPEG XT) × 5 levels = 100 pairs.
Target = reconstructed JND (AIC-3 PTC+BTC, 151 observers, 1000-nit displays, SDR white 100 cd/m²);
distortion-oriented (0 = identical). Local clone: `/mnt/v/datasets/aic-hdr2025`.

**Role.** T0, eval-only, frozen models only (see `docs/DATA_SPLITS.md`). Nothing was fitted or selected.

**What was scored.**
- zensim A: the PUBLISHED crate (`zensim` 0.3.0, imazen/zensim main `6060039d`), `Zensim::compute_hdr`
  with `HdrEncoding::Pq`, BT.2020 primaries, the PNG samples passed as PQ code values (zenpng decode, no
  colour conversion; the files' ICC is Rec2100PQ). Display peak 1000 nits (the test displays) as the primary
  row; 10000 nits as a sensitivity row. Tool: `tools/aichdr_score` (binary sha256 `ad607274…94b4c2`).
- Every other metric: the authors' own `data/metric_scores.csv`, not recomputed here.

**Statistics.** All from the canonical `panel` binary (`zensim_validate::panel`, Mohammadi 2025 panel:
SROCC, PLCC after the 4-parameter logistic, KROCC, OR, PWRC, Z-RMSE). Global = all 100 stimuli in one bucket
(the primary number). Within-source = mean of the five per-source SROCCs. CI = 2000-resample paired image
bootstrap (seed 20261114) through `panel --batch`. With only 5 sources the image bootstrap understates
between-content uncertainty; read the CIs as a lower bound. Driver: `scripts/aichdr/aichdr2025_eval.py`.

**Reproduction check.** The global SROCC and the per-source mean reproduce the paper's Table II for 25 of
the 29 published columns (e.g. HDR-VDP-2 0.946, SSIMULACRA 2 0.895 / 0.958, Butteraugli-pnorm 0.886 / 0.947,
CVVDP 0.753). Four columns do NOT: `SSIM` 0.600 vs Table II 0.672, `SSIM after TM` 0.660 vs 0.533,
`MS-SSIM (PQ)` 0.549 vs 0.731, `MS-SSIM_after TM` 0.538 vs 0.669. The released CSV's SSIM-family columns
differ from the paper; which is right is unknown.

## Reading

- zensim A ranks 6th of 31 globally: SROCC 0.845 [0.776, 0.886], PLCC 0.858, PWRC 0.972, OR 0.
  It ties Butteraugli-max (0.845) and beats CVVDP (0.753), HDR-VDP-3 (0.744), every PU21 and tone-mapped
  SSIM/MS-SSIM/IW-SSIM variant and VMAF.
- SSIMULACRA 2 (0.895) and Butteraugli-pnorm (0.886) beat A; both paired-bootstrap ΔSROCC intervals exclude
  zero (A−ssim2 −0.081…−0.022; A−butter-pnorm −0.071…−0.015). HDR-VDP-2 leads everything (0.946).
- Within-source A is 0.918 vs ssim2 0.958 and butter-pnorm 0.947. A's weakest source is alps-sunset (0.821
  vs ssim2 0.958); the other four are 0.914–0.965.
- The display peak hardly matters: 10000 nits gives 0.849 vs 0.845 at 1000 (ΔCI −0.013…+0.004).
- zensim A was fitted on SDR content; its HDR front end is the fixed 203-nit anchored cube root
  (`docs/PROFILES.md` in the zensim repo). This is the first human-JND HDR read of that public path.
