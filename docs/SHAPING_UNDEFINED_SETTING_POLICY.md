# Undefined human panels in base tuning

Approved by the owner on November 7, 2026, after observing collapsed frozen
product fits. This is a disclosed post-registration amendment, not a change to
the frozen PLAN or retrospective evidence of an advance-specified policy.

Policy identifier: `whole-setting-undefined-human-v1`.

Require every registered paired report for all four base settings. If any human
pooled or within-source correlation is undefined in any paired fit, mark that
entire setting ineligible. Retain all its fits and identify each undefined cell;
record its setting mean as null. Do not impute a score, average surviving seeds,
or reduce the paired coverage. Select among fully defined settings using the
unchanged equal-human composite and 1e-12 tie rule. Refuse selection when none
remain. Teacher scores and outer results cannot affect this decision.

An undefined final panel may retain its original hashed convergence-failure
receipt instead of a successful convergence receipt. Reproduce the diagnostic
failure and verify its complete paired diagnostic inputs and training exposure.
This remains an investigation failure, never a convergence qualification.
Finite final panels continue to require the existing convergence receipt.
Native correctness gates, final scheduled checkpoints, tuning budgets, seeds,
source partitions, rescue budgets and all numerical thresholds stay unchanged.

Selection receipts pin this policy and retain the eligibility reasons and all
report hashes. Existing receipts without this policy replay under their original
all-finite rule. The amendment applies to base-setting selection for rescue
admission; it does not silently amend rescue-choice or full-FIT selection.
