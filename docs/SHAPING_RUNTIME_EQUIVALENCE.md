# Exact-trajectory runtime substitution

This policy changes execution of unstarted cells only. It preserves the frozen
scientific matrix, source membership, feature definitions, label maps, seeds,
optimizer settings, training exposure, full-fold admission and final selection.
Running, completed and interrupted attempts keep their original identities.

The paired-gradient candidate may serve only joint-product/joint-separate
identity shaping and their joint pretrained variants. Other families retain the
existing runtime. Its three inference/evaluation executables must be byte-identical
to the registered corrected runtime; only the trainer executable differs.

Before activation, require every checkpoint of both registered 240-epoch identity
controls and both corrected 120-epoch pretrained controls to match exactly after
removing only `zentrain.repro`. Import the original pretrained shaping banks and
retain every original second-phase argument and pair budget. These four native
checks cover 720 checkpoints. The original corrected-initialization certificate
remains required and unchanged.

Archive the native results and reread complete durable checksums before issuing
a certificate. Bind its plan, baseline/candidate builds, all four cases, original
reference hashes, full comparison reports and archive manifests. Unknown families,
partial trajectories, changed hashes and missing evidence refuse admission.

Prepare the certificate separately from activation. Activate only after the new
registration and collection owners can reproduce it. Each worker capsule carries
the exact certificate and evidence pins. Reproduce its family/build binding
before native execution and terminal collection. A certificate maps the candidate
to its qualified baseline for the existing corrected-initialization gate; it
does not relabel the actual native build in any request, run, result or archive.

Keep the inner-selected evaluation representation and its original evaluator.
Do not regenerate completed scientific fits, amend undefined-score rules, widen
the shortlist, shorten a phase or bypass an incomplete paired selection. No model
promotion or serving-profile change follows from runtime equivalence.

Extension transport reproduces the complete fold's existing selection evidence.
The first fold-2 closure measured 1,647 files and 487,417,886 bytes before the
imported-source additions. Its former ordinary-neural 384 MiB allowance refused
before request creation or native training. Extensions now have a separate
1 GiB / 4,000-file input bound, reserved through the existing SSD admission.
Other neural transfers retain 384 MiB. No selection evidence is omitted and
the 30 GiB free-space floor remains unchanged.
